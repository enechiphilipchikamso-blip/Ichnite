What is Ichnite?

Ichnite is a Solana wallet checker. Paste any Solana wallet address and instantly view SOL balance, token holdings, NFTs and transaction history.

Is my wallet safe?

Yes. Ichnite is read-only. We never ask for your private key or seed phrase. We cannot access, move or modify your funds in any way.

Why is my balance not showing?

Make sure you have pasted a valid Solana wallet address. If the problem continues the Solana network may be experiencing delays. Try again shortly.

What wallets does Ichnite support?

Ichnite supports any valid Solana wallet address. Simply paste your address and Ichnite will do the rest.

Why is the search button temporarily disabled?

To prevent abuse, Ichnite temporarily limits how many searches can be made in a short period. If you see a countdown, wait for it to finish, then search again.

Can I install Ichnite or use it offline?

Yes, Ichnite can be installed as an app on your device. An internet connection is still required to fetch live wallet data.

Is my feedback anonymous?

Yes. The feedback box does not collect your name or email, we only receive the message you send.
