What is SolTrace?

SolTrace is a Solana wallet checker. Paste any Solana wallet address and instantly view SOL balance, token holdings, NFTs and transaction history.

Is my wallet safe?

Yes. SolTrace is read-only. We never ask for your private key or seed phrase. We cannot access, move or modify your funds in any way.

Why is my balance not showing?

Make sure you have pasted a valid Solana wallet address. If the problem continues the Solana network may be experiencing delays. Try again shortly.

What wallets does SolTrace support?

SolTrace supports any valid Solana wallet address. Simply paste your address and SolTrace will do the rest.
